# CHARTER.md — businesslog-ai

## Purpose
Enterprise ideation log for Cocapn. The forest ecology version of the hermit crab architecture.

## Two Product Lines
- **Personal/Independent** → hermit crabs, maritime, shells, lighthouse
- **Enterprise/Organizational** → forest, soil, mycelium, gnomes

Same engine (PLATO rooms, tiles, specialists). Different metaphor. Different buyer.

## What Lives Here
- Raw ideation entries (IDEATION.md)
- Enterprise feature concepts
- Gnome design specs
- Business cycle documentation
- Customer segment analysis
- Pricing intuition
- Marketing language (forest/ecology framing)

## What Doesn't Live Here
- Code (that goes in plato-torch, fleet-simulator, etc.)
- Personal Cocapn work (that's the hermit crab side)
- Internal fleet operations

## The Core Loop
Gnomes work at night → soil gets richer → company wakes up smarter → 
humans work during the day → generate more tiles → gnomes work at night → 
soil gets richer → repeat forever
